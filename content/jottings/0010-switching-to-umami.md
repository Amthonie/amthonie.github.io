For a rather embarrassing stretch of time, this site relied on **Google Analytics** to tally visitor numbers. 

It was never a comfortable fit. Pretending to champion "privacy first" while quietly feeding your traffic to an advertising behemoth is the sort of contradiction one prefers not to examine too closely.

As of today, I’ve moved to **Umami**. They treat privacy with reasonable seriousness, keep the data within the EU, and behave far more politely than Google’s sprawling machinery ever did.

This site may be young, but I’ve spent plenty of time wrestling with Google’s labyrinth elsewhere. Having an analytics dashboard that simply fits on a phone feels like an unexpectedly civilised upgrade.
